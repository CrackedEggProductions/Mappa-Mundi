# River End

## 1. Tile name

**River End** — production filename stem: `river_end`. Art status: **HAS EXAMPLE**.

## 2. Authoritative role

Basic Act-I Expansion; the same design serves River source or terminus. Starting bag: **2 copies**.

Source: [Complete Alpha Rules](../../../Carcassonne_Roguelite_Alpha_Complete_Rules.md), **RULE-CAT-005**; bag counts follow **RULE-BAG-002**. This brief defines art, not gameplay.

## 3. Edge occupancy

- North: **River**
- East: **Field**
- South: **Field**
- West: **Field**

This is the production orientation, cross-checked against the existing Homestead content definition; the catalogue defines the topology, not a new fixed play orientation. Rotate the whole tile in 90-degree steps during play; do not mirror it.

## 4. Internal feature relationships

One River component meets north and ends within the tile; east, south and west remain Field.

## 5. Visual communication goals

A centered north water ribbon leads to a restrained internal endpoint. Keep source/terminus reading neutral; no directional flow dependency. Follow the [master art recipe](../../specs/master_tile_art_spec.md): parchment first, fine sepia ink second, restrained color wash third. No scenic painting or 3D rendering.

## 6. Alignment requirements

- Use the [edge alignment specification](../../specs/tile_edge_alignment_spec.md): 1000 × 1000 design units, consistent edge clarity bands, no artwork frame that interrupts joins.
- River sockets: exact edge midpoint, 200-unit width (20%); distinct from the narrower Road standard.
- Keep Field boundaries open and visually quiet; no unlisted feature may acquire an edge exit. Area-feature corner endpoints do not create an extra Field-side connection.

## 7. Color wash guidance

- River: faint desaturated light blue with clear sepia banks.
- Field: faint desaturated light green; keep the parchment visible.

Wash stays low-saturation and translucent. Do not use decorative color to imply an unlisted gameplay feature.

## 8. Reference images to use

Inspect the entire [approved style set](../../references/approved_style/) first, then these relevant studies:

- [15 river end](../../references/approved_style/ref_15_river_end.png) — style anchor; [mechanical study](../../references/approved_mechanical/ref_15_river_end.png) (conceptual topology only; recheck sockets).

North water endpoint/pool is a useful match. Example orientation/width is subordinate to this brief and the alignment spec; “approved reference” does not mean final production art.

## 9. Forbidden mistakes

Do not create a separate River Source design, extra water exit, coastline or lake occupying another boundary. Do not copy texture borders, off-center sockets or topology defects from a reference. Never let scenery imply additional feature exits.
